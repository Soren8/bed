Longevity logic: the core must outlive the comfort

Core = support

The firm layer underneath. It must stay flat longer than anything above it.

Topper = comfort

The soft layer on top, inside the same cover. It wears out first; plan to replace it.

Cover = holds together

One knit case around core and topper together. Order depth = core + topper (6+2 → 8″); adds no support.

Base = wood

Flat wood slats that hold the weight. Lasts decades.

How long materials last

  • Natural latex 12–20 yr — longest; firm Dunlop (37–44 ILD) is standard core firmness, on-top feel.
  • Pocket-coil support, durable — hybrid tracks its comfort layer (~7–12 yr).
  • High-density foam ~6–10 yr — durable for foam.
  • Budget 1.5–1.8 PCF polyfoam ~3–6 yr — shortest.

Ranges from Sleep Foundation durability methodology, Tom’s Guide, and the Avocado help center — industry consensus and lab testing, not peer-reviewed guarantees; comfort layers wear out first.

Value means yearly cost: stack total ÷ expected years of service. A cheap stack that sags in 3 years costs more per year than a pricier one that lasts 12. The cheap-foam-trap: a foam core under foam — a 1.8 lb/ft³ foam core softens at about the same rate as a foam topper, so the two sag together with nothing firm underneath. When foam still makes sense: the cheapest ticket in — budget Regular + thin topper, eyes open that coil wins per year.

DIY, method & watchlist

2×4 base plan — 19 boards, $72–$120

Cut list

  • 2 side rails — 80″ (on edge)
  • 2 end rails — 73″ (between sides)
  • 1 center beam — 77″ (on edge)
  • 14 slats — 76″ (flat, gaps ≈2.39″)

Buy 96″ boards, not 92⅝″ studs. Slat spans ≈35.75″. Shop project, not engineering guidance.

Options

  • Reuse base — $0 if flat & supportive
  • Retrofit slats — $60–$85 est.

Method & fine print
  • Research synthesis, not hands-on testing of every combo. Firmness feel varies.
  • Trust rule: every pick is an established maker (IKEA, Latex Mattress Factory, Lucid, Sleep Innovations, Linenspa), an Amazon listing shipped by Amazon with free 90-day returns, or a direct specialty seller that publishes full specs (Foam Factory lists density + ILD for every foam). A pick gets demoted the moment its buy flow leaves the trusted site or its stock can't be verified — see the Lux-HQ watchlist entry.
  • Outbound links are ordinary links (rel="noopener"). Totals are arithmetic on snapshot prices.
  • Foam Factory: restrictive returns, no generous sleep trial; free contiguous-US shipping on qualifying $100+ orders.
  • Bare foam + fabric cover is not verified here as a fire-tested finished mattress.
  • Density (lb/ft³) and firmness (ILD) are different numbers. Firm Dunlop 37–44 ILD (Sleeping Organic scale: Firm 37–40, X-Firm 44) is standard support-core firmness.
  • Longevity ranges (latex 12–20 yr, hybrid ~7–12 yr, high-density foam ~6–10 yr, budget polyfoam ~3–6 yr) are summarized from Sleep Foundation durability methodology, Tom’s Guide, and the Avocado help center — ranges, not guarantees. Exact year figures are industry consensus and lab testing, not peer-reviewed guarantees.
  • Why latex leads: vulcanized latex is cross-linked rubber that springs back after compression; memory foam and polyfoam accumulate compression set as cell walls collapse. Dense Dunlop outlasts softer Talalay, so Dunlop is the core choice here.
